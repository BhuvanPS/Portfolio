The Retail Reality Gap

Modern retail is plagued by a disconnect between demand signals and inventory action. Traditional systems react too late.

Hidden Stockouts

High-demand items vanish from shelves before systems update. Customers see "Out of Stock" physically while the system says available.

Reactive Latency

Operations teams rely on 24-hour old reports. By the time a trend is spotted, the revenue opportunity is already lost.

Siloed Data

Historical sales data and real-time streams live in different worlds, making it impossible to correlate immediate trends with past performance.
